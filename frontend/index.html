About FundiConnect

Built for Kenya's growing local service economy

FundiConnect helps homeowners, landlords, businesses, and families discover reliable local professionals across Kenya. Our mission is simple: help skilled Kenyans grow their businesses while making trusted services easier to access.

From quick repairs to home upgrades, we connect people with the right professionals for the job and support a more transparent, confident local marketplace.
